# Chase landscape artwork

Original vector drawings created for the Chase interface: moonlit contours and distant towers, open sky and water contours, warm sunlit terrain, botanical leaves, and a cosmic variation. All use solid fills and strokes, with no gradients or animation. They are decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Real card images are separate bundled references and remain uncropped. The five scenes are art palettes within one light interface, not user-selectable app themes.
